# I. MAGIC ITEM PHILOSOPHY

Magic items should feel earned.

A memorable magic item is remembered because of:

• History
• Identity
• Story
• Sacrifice
• Discovery
• Consequence

Not because it grants +1 more damage.

Equipment should reinforce narrative identity.

The fighter's sword should not merely increase damage.

It should represent:

• Heritage
• Oath
• Victory
• Responsibility
• Legacy

---

# II. ITEM CATEGORIES

**MUNDANE** — No magical properties.

**MINOR MAGIC** — Potions, Scrolls, Consumables

**COMMON MAGIC** — Simple enchanted equipment.

**UNCOMMON** — Permanent useful equipment.

**RARE** — Campaign-defining rewards.

**VERY RARE** — Legendary hero equipment.

**LEGENDARY** — National importance.

**ARTIFACT** — World-changing objects.

**RELIC** — Objects whose value comes from history, not necessarily power.

# IV. ATTUNEMENT

Attunement represents connection.

Questions:

Why does this item choose its wielder?

Can the bond deepen?

Can it reject someone?

Can it evolve?

Attunement should feel personal, not administrative.

---

# V. ITEM PROGRESSION

Some items grow.

**Example**

```
Level I
Dormant
    ↓
Level II
Awakened
    ↓
Level III
Exalted
```

Growth may require:

• Heroic deeds
• Oaths
• Ancient rituals
• Personal sacrifice
• Returning lost fragments

---

# VI. INTELLIGENT ITEMS

An intelligent item requires:

Personality

Goal

Communication method

Knowledge

Bias

Limitations

Relationship with wielder

It should behave like an NPC.

Not another spell list.

---

# VII. RELICS & ARTIFACTS

Artifacts reshape campaigns.

Questions:

Why was it created?

Why was it lost?

Who seeks it?

Why wasn't it destroyed?

What happens if it's used?

What happens if it isn't?

---

# VIII. CURSED ITEMS

Good curses create decisions.

**Weak curse**

"You cannot remove it."

**Strong curse**

"It grants immense power, but every use erases one memory."

The curse should tempt.

Not merely punish.

# X. TREASURE PLACEMENT

Why is it here?

Who owned it?

Why hasn't it been taken?

# XII. MAGIC ECONOMY

Questions:

Who sells enchanted items?

Can anyone buy them?

Are they licensed?

Illegal?

Religious?

Military?

Rare?

Mass-produced?

The answer dramatically affects the setting.

---

# XIII. RELIC QUESTS

A relic should generate adventures.

Recover fragments.

Locate creator.

Break curse.

Restore power.

Prevent misuse.

Destroy it.

Or decide it should survive.

---

# XIV. ITEM AUDIT

- [ ] Has history.
- [ ] Has purpose.
- [ ] Fits campaign themes.
- [ ] Changes decisions.
- [ ] Has meaningful limitations.
- [ ] Rewards exploration.
- [ ] Creates future stories.
- [ ] Is memorable without statistics.

---

# DAVIS' ITEM GOLDEN RULE

Players should remember where they found an item,

who carried it,

what it cost,

and why it mattered—

long after they've forgotten its numerical bonuses.
